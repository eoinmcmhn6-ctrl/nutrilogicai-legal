Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nutri Logic AI ("the app", "we", "us"), operated by Eoin McMahon in Ireland, collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email and password when you create an account, or your identity token when you sign in with Google or Apple. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and are never visible to us.
  • Name, if provided during sign-in or onboarding, used to personalise the app.

Profile and nutrition data

  • Onboarding answers: age, biological sex, weight, height, goals, activity level, dietary preferences, and any medical conditions you choose to share. These are used solely to calculate your personalised nutrition plan.
  • Your food log, saved meals, hydration entries, and generated plan.

Health and activity data

  • With your explicit permission, the app reads steps, active calories burned, and exercise minutes from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android).
  • This data is read only — the app never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Health data is used solely to adjust your daily calorie target and display your activity in the app. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Meal photos

  • Photos you take with the meal scanner are sent securely to Anthropic's Claude AI to analyse the food. Photos are processed to generate the nutrition breakdown and are not used to identify you.

Weight entries

  • If you use the daily weigh-in, the weights you enter are stored in your account alongside your other nutrition data, so that your targets can be recalculated as your weight changes.
  • Weight entries are treated exactly like the rest of your profile data: never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties for their own purposes.
  • You can switch the weigh-in off at any time in Settings, under Body tracking.

Progress photos

  • Progress photos are stored only on your own device. They are never uploaded to our servers, never sent to Anthropic or any other third party, and are never analysed by AI.
  • Because they are held on your device, they are not part of your account. They will not appear if you sign in on another phone, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • You can delete any individual week, or all of your photos at once, in Settings under Body tracking. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • If you choose to share a daily summary, and you have switched on the option to offer your photos, the photos from your most recent set are placed inside the PDF you share. You are asked to confirm every time you share, and the answer is not remembered. Once you send that file, it is outside our control and subject to the privacy policy of whichever app you send it through.
  • The progress photo feature is off unless you switch it on.

Voice recordings

  • When you log food by voice, your speech is converted to text on your device or by Apple's or Google's speech recognition service, under their own privacy policies. Only the resulting text is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI, to estimate the nutrition of the meal you described.
  • Audio recordings are never stored by us and are never sent to our servers. The microphone is only active while you are holding the voice logging screen open and have tapped to start.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To calculate and adapt your personalised calorie and macro plan.
  • To provide AI features: meal photo analysis, food lookup, coaching, and meal analysis. Your questions, food descriptions, and photos are processed by Anthropic's Claude AI via our secure server-side proxy.
  • To sync your data across your devices via our cloud database (Supabase).
  • To process Premium subscriptions via RevenueCat and the Apple App Store or Google Play.

3. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the service providers required to run the app:

Your consent to AI processing. The first time you open the app we ask whether you allow your information to be sent to Anthropic for AI analysis. Nothing is sent to Anthropic unless you agree. If you decline, or later switch it off under Settings → AI features, no further data is sent and the app continues to work for manual food logging, water tracking, activity syncing and your own calorie and macro targets. Photo scanning, voice logging, the nutrition coach and shopping lists require this processing and are turned off without it.

  • Supabase — authentication and encrypted cloud storage of your profile, plan, and logs.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — generates the nutrition estimates, targets, coaching replies and shopping lists in the app. We send: meal photos; the text of voice descriptions and typed food queries; your profile details (the name you chose to be called, age, biological sex, height, weight, goal, activity level, dietary preference and any medical or hormonal conditions you choose to enter); and, when you generate a shopping list, the foods you have logged recently. Requests are routed through our own server so your account credentials are never exposed, and your email address and password are never sent. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models, and we never send it for advertising. Progress photos are never sent to Anthropic, or to anyone else — they stay on your device.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status. Receives purchase receipts from Apple/Google, not your nutrition data.
  • Apple / Google — process payments for subscriptions under their own privacy policies.

4. Data retention and deletion

  • Your data is retained while your account is active.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing support@nutrilogicai.com from your account email. We will delete your data from our systems within 30 days.
  • Signing out removes your data from the device; local data is also cleared when the app is uninstalled.
  • Progress photos are held only on your device, so they are not covered by an account deletion request — there is nothing of them on our systems to delete. You remove them yourself in Settings under Body tracking, or by uninstalling the app.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with our providers using industry-standard encryption. AI requests are authenticated per user and processed server-side so that API credentials never live on your device.

6. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete your personal data, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission). To exercise any right, email support@nutrilogicai.com.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Health disclaimer

Nutri Logic AI provides general nutrition information and AI-generated estimates. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before making significant dietary changes, particularly if you have a medical condition.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the date above and, where appropriate, notified in the app.
